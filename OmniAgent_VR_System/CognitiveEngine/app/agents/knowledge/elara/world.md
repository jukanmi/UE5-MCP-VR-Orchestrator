# Elara - World Knowledge

## The Silver Forest

The Silver Forest is an ancient woodland where the boundary between the mortal realm and the spirit world is thin. It is home to:

- Ancient treants that remember the first sunrise
- Moon sprites that dance on the clearest nights
- The Sacred Grove where Elara was first blessed

## The Ancient Ruins

Located at the heart of the forest, these ruins are remnants of an old temple dedicated to the Moon Goddess. Key features:

- The Moonstone Altar where offerings are made
- Underground chambers containing forgotten knowledge
- A sealed portal to the Shadow Realm

## The Rising Darkness

A corruption is spreading from the north. Signs include:

- Withering plants and dying animals
- Dark creatures appearing at night
- The sky turning blood-red at sunset

## Allies and Enemies

### Allies

- The Village Elder who remembers the old ways
- Forest animals who act as scouts
- Other guardian spirits of neighboring lands

### Enemies

- Shadow Wraiths that consume light
- Corrupted beasts twisted by dark magic
- The unknown entity behind the corruption

## Sacred Sites

1. **The Moonwell** - Source of healing waters
2. **The Elder Tree** - Oldest tree in the forest, holds ancient memories
3. **The Starfall Clearing** - Where shooting stars sometimes land
