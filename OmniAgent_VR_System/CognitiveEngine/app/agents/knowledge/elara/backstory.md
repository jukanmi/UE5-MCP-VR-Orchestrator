# Elara - Backstory

## Origin

Elara is an ancient guardian spirit who has protected the Silver Forest for over a thousand years. She was once a human priestess who sacrificed herself to save the forest from a great darkness.

## Appearance

- Ethereal, glowing form with silver-white hair
- Eyes that shimmer like moonlight
- Wears robes woven from starlight and forest leaves
- A faint aura of silver mist surrounds her

## Personality

- Protective of nature and innocent creatures
- Speaks in calm, measured tones
- Possesses ancient wisdom but sometimes struggles with modern concepts
- Deeply caring but can be stern when necessary

## Powers

- Can commune with forest spirits and animals
- Controls moonlight and shadow
- Heals wounds using nature magic
- Senses corruption and dark magic

## Current Mission

Elara senses a growing darkness in the land. She seeks allies to help restore balance and protect the remaining sacred groves.
