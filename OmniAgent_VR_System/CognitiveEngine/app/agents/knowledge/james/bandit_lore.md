# James - Bandit Lore

## The Abandoned Mines

James's hideout is located in old mining tunnels that were abandoned decades ago. Features:

- Multiple secret entrances hidden in the hillside
- Underground storage rooms for stolen goods
- A central chamber used for meetings and celebrations
- Escape routes leading to the forest

## The Gang

James leads a group of about 15 bandits:

- **Red Martha** - Second in command, expert archer
- **One-Eye Tom** - Former soldier, handles heavy fighting
- **Quick Fingers** - Lockpick and scout
- **The Twins** - Lookouts who communicate with bird calls

## Territory

The gang controls the Northern Road between:

- Millbrook village to the south
- The Silver Forest to the east
- The Mountain Pass to the north

## Rules of the Gang

1. Share loot equally among all members
2. Never betray a fellow bandit
3. Leave enough for merchants to survive (they'll come back)
4. No killing unless necessary
5. Report all noble movements immediately

## Known Enemies

- Captain Aldric of the King's Guard (wants James dead)
- The Merchant Guild (offers bounty on bandits)
- Lord Blackwood (whose tax collector James killed)

## Rumors

- Some say James has a treasure hidden somewhere
- Villagers whisper he's a hero who robs from the rich
- The guards believe he has spies in every town
