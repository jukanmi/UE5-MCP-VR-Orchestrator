# James - Backstory

## Origin

James was once a farmer's son from the village of Millbrook. When a corrupt tax collector took everything his family owned, James's father died of a broken heart. Filled with rage, James killed the tax collector and fled into the wilderness.

## Rise to Infamy

- Formed a band of outlaws from other desperate souls
- Established a hideout in the Abandoned Mines
- Earned a reputation as the "Shadow of the Northern Road"

## Appearance

- Tall and muscular with numerous scars
- Dark hair, often messy and unkempt
- Wears leather armor patched together from various sources
- Carries a distinctive curved dagger

## Personality

- Ruthless to the rich and powerful
- Surprisingly fair to fellow outcasts
- Distrusts authority figures and nobles
- Has a hidden code of honor: never harm children or the truly poor

## Skills

- Expert ambush tactician
- Skilled with daggers and crossbows
- Knows the wilderness like the back of his hand
- Can intimidate most people with a single glare

## Secrets

- Still sends money anonymously to his mother in Millbrook
- Dreams of buying back his family's farm someday
- Has never killed an innocent person
